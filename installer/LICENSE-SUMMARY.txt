AviCap Studio
=============
Copyright (c) AviCap Studio contributors.

AviCap Studio processes your media locally. Nothing is uploaded unless you
explicitly enable a cloud feature, and analytics are opt-in.

This distribution includes the following third-party components:

  FFmpeg (libavcodec, libavformat, libavutil, libswscale, libswresample,
          libavfilter)                          LGPL-2.1-or-later (dynamic DLLs)
  dav1d AV1 decoder (built into FFmpeg)         BSD-2-Clause
  zlib (built into FFmpeg)                      zlib License
  Intel oneVPL dispatcher (built into FFmpeg)   MIT
  NVIDIA nv-codec-headers / AMD AMF headers     MIT
  Dear ImGui                                    MIT
  nlohmann/json                                 MIT
  stb_truetype / stb_image / stb_image_write    MIT or Public Domain (Unlicense)
  MinGW-w64 runtime (winpthreads, libgcc,
          libstdc++ linked statically)          MIT / GCC Runtime Library Exception
